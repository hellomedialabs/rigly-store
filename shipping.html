Shipping

Processing

Orders are processed within 1 to 3 business days.

Most orders arrive 6 to 14 business days after you order. Some take up to 20. Items ship from overseas warehouses, and delays can happen at customs or during busy seasons.

You will receive a tracking number by email once your order ships. Tracking may take a few days to show updates.

Depending on where you live, customs or import fees may apply. These are the buyer's responsibility.
