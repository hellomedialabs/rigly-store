Shipping

Processing time

Orders are processed within 1 to 3 business days.

Delivery time

Most orders arrive within 8 to 20 business days after processing. Items ship from overseas warehouses, so delivery takes longer than from a local retailer. Delays can happen during busy seasons or at customs.

Tracking

You will receive a tracking number by email once your order ships. Tracking may take a few days to show updates.

Customs and duties

Depending on your country, customs or import fees may apply. These are the buyer's responsibility.
