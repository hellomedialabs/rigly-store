Returns and Refunds

30-day window

If you are not happy with your order, contact us within 30 days of delivery.

Damaged or wrong items

If your item arrives damaged, defective or not as described, contact us through our Contact page with your order number and a photo. We will send a replacement or a refund.

Change of mind

Items must be unused and in original condition. Return shipping costs are paid by the buyer. Contact us first and do not send anything back without a return address from us.

Refunds

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.
